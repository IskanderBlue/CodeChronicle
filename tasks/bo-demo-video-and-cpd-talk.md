# The demo video, and the talk that comes out of it

**Prefix:** `b-` — medium priority, actionable now. No new code is needed.
Nothing here needs you on camera.

## Goal

One screen recording, about 10 minutes, with your voice over your own screen.
It becomes three things at once:

1. The demo on the landing page, for a visitor who will not sign up first.
2. The attachment in outreach email, which is why the chapter links matter.
3. The draft script for a live lunch-and-learn, and later for CPD approval.

**Chapters are the point.** Nobody watches 10 minutes of a tool they have not
bought. They watch the 90 seconds that answers their question. Every chapter
must stand alone and have its own link.

## What you need

- A screen recorder. OBS Studio is free and records to MP4.
- A microphone that is not the laptop's. A USB headset is enough. Bad audio
  loses more viewers than a bad face, and there is no face here.
- A quiet room, and 40 minutes.
- A browser window at 1400 x 900. Bigger text is easier to read on a phone.
- Light mode. The site's dark mode is good, but a screen recording in dark
  mode washes out on a projector at a lunch-and-learn.

## Before you record

- Log in as a Pro account, so no gate interrupts the demonstration.
- Choose the provision first. Use the one from
  `tasks/a-proof-content-edition-comparison.md`, so the video and the article
  tell the same story.
- Write the chapter list below onto a card. Do not write a full script. A read
  script sounds read.
- Do one full practice run and throw it away.

## The chapters

Record the whole thing in one take, in this order, and pause between chapters.
A pause gives you a clean cut point and a clean timestamp.

| # | Title | Length | Content |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The problem | 0:45 | A permit was issued in 1999. Which text applies? Say what getting it wrong costs. |
| 2 | One search | 1:30 | Type the question with a date. Show the result. Do not explain the interface. |
| 3 | The date is the control | 1:30 | Move the AS-OF date. Show the same provision change with the date. |
| 4 | What changed, and which regulation changed it | 2:00 | Open the amendment chain. Name the amending regulation. |
| 5 | The 1997 edition | 1:30 | Show a page scan. Say plainly that this text is not on e-Laws. |
| 6 | How to check us | 1:30 | The provenance rail, the Sources page, the external source links. Invite disagreement. |
| 7 | What it costs | 0:45 | Free tier, Pro, and what Pro adds. No pressure. |

Chapter 5 is the one that sells. Do not rush it.

## Voice

- Say what you are about to do, then do it. Do not narrate the mouse.
- Use the words a code consultant uses. Say "provision", not "section".
- Do not apologise for anything on screen. If it is wrong, cut it.
- Silence is fine. Fill it in the edit, not in the room.

## Publishing

- Upload to YouTube as **Unlisted** first. Watch it once. Then make it public.
- Put the chapter list in the description with timestamps at the start of each
  line, in `M:SS` form, starting at `0:00`. YouTube reads that format and
  draws the chapter bar automatically. The first timestamp must be `0:00` or
  no chapters appear.
- Get the per-chapter links. A YouTube link takes `&t=` in seconds, for
  example `https://youtu.be/XXXX?t=105`. Collect one link per chapter and
  keep the list in this file when the video is done.
- Turn subtitles on. YouTube's automatic captions are good enough, but read
  them once: they mistake provision numbers.

## Where the links go

- Chapter 1 and 2: the landing page demo.
- Chapter 5: the outreach email, and the proof-content article.
- Chapter 6: the verification-rail guide page.
- The full video: the pricing page.

## The live talk, later

The same seven chapters are the talk. Offer it first as a free
lunch-and-learn to a code consultancy or a law firm. Pursue formal CPD
(Continuing Professional Development) approval with the OBOA, OAA or PEO only
after the talk has worked twice in a room. Approval is paperwork, and
paperwork for a talk that does not land is wasted.

## Done when

- The video is public, with chapters, and the per-chapter links are recorded
  in this file.
- Chapter links are placed on the landing page and the pricing page.
- The video is sent to at least 20 people by direct email.
