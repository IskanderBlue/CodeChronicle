How high does a guard on a house stair have to be?

Seven texts since 1998. The code sets the flight and the landing separately, and for eight years they were 170 mm apart.

Retrieved {{ retrieved|date:"j F Y" }}. Every text below is rendered from our corpus by the components the product ships, except the 2024 one, which is quoted.

What is the minimum height of a guard on a stair inside a house in Ontario?

The answer depends on the date, and on which part of the stair you are asking about. A flight is the sloped run of steps. A landing is the flat platform at the top, at the foot, or between two flights. The code has set them apart and they have at times varied. For a time starting in 2022, spiral stairs were handled separately.

A building is governed by the code in force when its permit was issued, so the answer varies by date.

Note, we are assuming the stairs are not exit stairs, so their 920 mm rule does not apply.

1998: 800 mm

OBC1997 came into force on 6 April 1998. Guard height is Article 9.8.8.2., and sentence (4) answers our question. It sets 800 mm for indoor stairs in general and 900 mm above their landings.

31 December 2006: the flight rises, and the article shifts

OBC2006 rewrites the article. Article 9.8.8.2. still exists, and it is now Loads on Guards. Our rule now falls under Article 9.8.8.3. and the 800 mm allowance is dropped. Sentence (2) now covers every guard within a dwelling unit, landings included.

1 January 2014: the landing goes to 1 070 mm

OBC2012 changes several things, but relevant to our question is sentence (5) which says guards shall be not less than (a) 920 mm for required exit stairs [not applicable to our case inside a house], and (b) 1 070 mm around landings.

1 July 2017: outside only

Only one sentence changes, and it's about exterior guards; "serving not more than one dwelling unit" becomes "serving a house or an individual dwelling unit". Not relevant to this question.

1 January 2022: landings drop back to 900 mm

Sentence (5) is revoked. No exception sets a landing height any more, so a landing inside a house falls to sentence (2) at 900 mm.

Words are also added to sentence (2): guards within dwelling units are 900 mm, other than guards serving spiral stairs. So, spiral stairs fall back to the general 1 070 mm requirement from sentence (1).

1 January 2025: the code in force today

O. Reg. 163/24 is one page. It adopts the National Building Code of Canada 2020, as amended by a separate document that the ministry re-issues under a new date whenever it changes. Guard height keeps the number 9.8.8.3. Ontario replaces one sentence and adds another.

OBC2024 is not in our corpus yet due to copyright, so this is the one text on the page we quote instead of render.

9.8.8.3. Height of Guards (See Note A-9.8.3.3.)

(1) Except as provided in Sentences (2) to (3.1), all guards shall be not less than 1 070 mm high.

(2) All guards within dwelling units or within houses with a secondary suite including their common spaces shall be not less than 900 mm high.

(3) Exterior guards serving not more than one dwelling unit or a house with a secondary suite including their common spaces shall be not less than 900 mm high where the walking surface served by the guard is not more than 1 800 mm above the finished ground level.

(3.1) The height of guards for exterior stairs and landings more than 10 m above adjacent ground level shall be not less than 1 500 mm.

(4) The height of guards for flights of steps shall be measured vertically from the top of the guard to a line drawn through the tread nosing served by the guard.

Article 9.8.8.3. as printed in the 2024 Building Code Compendium, Division B, Part 9. Sentences (1) and (3.1) are Ontario's, from the Ontario Amendments to the National Building Code of Canada 2020, 17 July 2026, sections 344(1) and 344(2), which is the document O. Reg. 163/24 currently adopts. The rest is the National Building Code of Canada 2020, which Ontario does not amend. © King's Printer for Ontario, 2024. Reproduced with permission.

Sentence (2) answers our question: 900 mm on flights, landings, and since they no longer have an exception, spiral stairs.
