The content comes from the same partials the reading page uses, so the exhibit cannot show something the product does not — that is the rule this export is built to. And the footer already carries the line this document must carry: "Not legal advice · Citation-grade reference · Verify against official consolidation". The header states everything an exhibit needs to stand on its own: the edition, the provision, the window it governed, the instrument behind it, and when this copy was taken.
