Is the ledger recording?

The ledger never raises, so it cannot report its own failure. This compares it against the provision-view events, which a different function writes. A day with signed-in views and no ledger write is the fault; both at zero only means nobody read anything.

Days answer the total outage. Partial failures answer the other one: each bulk surface stamps how many texts it handed over beside how many reached the ledger, so a page that delivered forty and recorded three is caught on the request rather than hidden inside a day that also has writes. Any number here above zero is a bug.
