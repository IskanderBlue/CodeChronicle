{% comment %} The two jobs a printable page needs from the browser. First: a crop's wrapper needs a height, and a height needs the image's own proportions. CCM ships no dimensions with page_images, so they are read here as each image loads. The wrapper is w x imgW wide and h x imgH tall, so its aspect ratio is the ratio of those. Second: printing before the images have reported their proportions prints a gap where the provision should be, so the button waits for them. A reader who presses Ctrl-P on a finished page is fine either way; the button exists for the reader who presses it the moment the page appears. {% endcomment %}
