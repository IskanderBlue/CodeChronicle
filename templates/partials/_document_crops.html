{% comment %} A provision's page images, cropped to the provision. The export form of partials/_document_images.html. On screen a scan is shown whole with the provision highlighted, because a reader wants to see it in its setting. In an exhibit the setting is somebody else's text, so this shows the marked region and nothing else. Expects: crops (core.page_crops.build_crops), alt_text. The crop is CSS, not a processed image: an overflow-hidden wrapper with the whole page positioned inside it, all offsets in percentages so they hold at any printed size. Percentages resolve against the wrapper — left against its width, top against its height — which is why the two offsets are divided by different numbers upstream. The wrapper's aspect ratio is the one value CSS cannot supply: it needs the image's own proportions and CCM ships no dimensions with page_images. The script below reads them off each image as it loads. Until then the wrapper has no height and the crop is invisible rather than wrong — a half-laid-out exhibit is worse than one that appears a moment later. break-inside: avoid keeps a crop off a sheet boundary. Do NOT put these in a scroll box: a max-height that clips on screen clips in print too, and prints the top of a provision as though it were all of it. {% endcomment %}
