The row you click in is the pin that moves, and there is no rule to get wrong. Three marks, one meaning each: filled circle this side is on this version quiet outline circle the other side holds it — a real option but for that strong outline circle a click moves this side here The strong outline is reserved for the only mark a click does anything to. Each row's date follows its own dot, on its own line. A date at the end of a row labels the row, and a row is not what the reader is reading.
